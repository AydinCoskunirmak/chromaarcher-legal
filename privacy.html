Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aydın Coşkunırmak ("we", "us", "our", "the Developer") handles information in connection with the Chroma Archer mobile game (the "App"). We built Chroma Archer to be simple: the App does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect or transmit personal information to us.

Short version: Chroma Archer doesn't ask for your name, email, or any personal details, and it doesn't have a server that stores your data. Your progress lives only on your own device.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information. The App has no login, no user profiles, no friends list, and no chat or social features.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your gameplay progress locally on your device only — things like which cities and levels you've unlocked, your stars, and your in-game gold balance. This is stored using your device's standard local app storage and is never sent to us, because we do not operate any server that receives it. If you delete the App or clear its data, this information is permanently lost and we cannot recover it for you.

3. In-app purchases

Chroma Archer may offer optional in-app purchases, such as in-game gold packs or a "Remove Ads" purchase. All payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play, not by us. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The store platform may share limited, non-identifying purchase confirmation data with the App (such as which item was bought) so we can grant it to you — this is handled through the store's own purchase system and is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

4. Advertising

As of the date above, Chroma Archer does not display third-party advertising. If we introduce ads in the future (for example, through a provider such as Google AdMob), those providers may collect technical identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID) to show and measure ads, subject to their own privacy policies — and, where required (such as under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework), we will ask for your permission first. We will update this page before any such change takes effect, and the App will keep offering a "Remove Ads" option.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

The App does not currently integrate any analytics or crash-reporting service. If we add one in the future, we will disclose it here and describe what it collects.

6. Children's privacy

Chroma Archer is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children — the App simply does not collect personal information in the first place. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the email below and we will address it.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, because we do not collect any information that could be shared. The only third parties involved with the App are Apple and Google, who process purchases as described above under their own privacy policies.

8. Data retention and your choices

Because gameplay data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it — uninstalling the App or clearing its storage removes it completely. Purchase records are retained by Apple or Google under their own policies, not by us.

9. Security

We take reasonable care in how the App is built. Since we do not operate servers that store your personal data, most of the usual server-side risks don't apply. No method of storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee the absolute security of data your operating system stores on your device.

10. International users

Chroma Archer is available worldwide. Because we do not collect personal data, there is no cross-border transfer of personal data by us to worry about; purchases are handled by Apple's or Google's own regional systems.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves — for example, if we add advertising, analytics, or new features. We'll post the updated version here and update the "Last updated" date above. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us
